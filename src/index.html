What makes it more than a chatbot

It remembers. It sees. It grows.

The system this team comes from has three powers. Your team has the same three, and they work for you.

Soul

It keeps what matters to you.

Your decisions, your tastes, the way you like things done: kept, each linked to where it came from. You are never asked twice.

Vision

It sees before it speaks.

Before anyone on your team answers, they are shown what the team already knows that bears on what you asked. So the answer fits you, not everyone.

Hindsight

Every day leaves it wiser.

After the work it looks back: what failed first, what fixed it, what you wished it had done. The lesson is kept, and tomorrow starts from there.

And it is yours to see. Ask it what it knows about you. Tell it to forget.

Proven

Nothing lands unproven.

Most AI code arrives as a claim that it works. Here a claim is where a change starts, and it carries no colour until it is proven.

  1. ClaimYou ask for something.
  2. JudgeThe Steward writes the test that defines done.
  3. RedIt has to fail first, or it proves nothing.
  4. GreenOperations builds until the same judge passes.
  5. ReceiptThe proof is recorded against the exact bytes.
  6. LandThe Master lands it on the receipt.

Context Reset

Sheds 61% of its context at every reset.

Every turn sends the model everything the session holds, and a session only gets heavier. At a safe break the team resets: a fresh session starts and only a small packet crosses over. On a local model that is the difference between a quick reply and a slow one.

  • The packetWhat is owed, what is in flight, and where to find the rest.
  • The checkAfter every reset the seat proves it can still reach what it needs.
  • The memoryEach packet is kept, and becomes hindsight the whole team can search.

Measured on our own system, 4 to 11 Nov 2026: 726 resets across four seats. A seat carried 231K tokens before a reset and 95K after (medians), and sent 47% less over the week than it would have without its latest reset.

It measures itself

The right model for each step

A plan comes first. Each step then goes to the smallest model whose record on that kind of step clears the bar. On your machine that record is measured: every model is timed and put through an agent check before it joins the team.
